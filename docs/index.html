控制範圍的但書：資料範圍內與分佈外Limits of the control: in-range vs outside the data

同一模型、同一情緒（憤怒）、同一句英文文本，僅改變所指定的激發度。第一格是訓練資料幾乎未出現的極端設定，其餘兩格落在訓練資料實際涵蓋的範圍內。第一格與其餘兩格的差異明顯，但那是模型在分佈外的外插行為；資料範圍內（第二、三格）的差異小得多——這正是客觀數值與聽感之間落差的來源。Same model, same emotion (angry), same English sentence; only the requested arousal changes. The first clip uses a setting the training data barely contains; the other two fall inside the range the data actually covers. The first clip clearly differs from the other two, but that is the model extrapolating outside its data: within the covered range (clips two and three) the difference is far smaller, which is where the gap between the objective numbers and what one hears comes from.

資料擴充前後的中文可懂度Mandarin intelligibility, before and after data expansion

擴充前的模型在中文上有明顯的遺忘現象，約四分之一的句子被合成為與文本無關的內容；擴充後幾乎消失。每格附上以 Whisper large-v2 轉寫所得的字元錯誤率與辨識結果。Before the expansion the model had clearly forgotten Mandarin: about a quarter of the sentences came out as content unrelated to the input text. Each cell shows the character error rate and the transcript from Whisper large-v2.
