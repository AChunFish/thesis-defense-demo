只給情緒類別，不給連續維度Emotion category only, no continuous dimensions

與上一區同一個模型，但情緒僅由情緒類別下達，不另給連續維度：激發度／支配度／愉悅度三維條件完全不給（填 nan），引導係數仍為 w=1。這不是權宜之計——訓練資料中的中文 ESD 真人語料（17,150 句）本來就是只有情緒類別、沒有連續標註，恐懼與厭惡的擴充語料亦然，因此七個類別在訓練時都見過這個條件。卡片上的激發度是情緒辨識模型聽合成結果回標的量測值，不是輸入設定——因為此處並沒有輸入任何激發度。另註：whisper 在悲傷與憤怒兩段各標出一個字的差異（「天空」→「青空」、「雲」→「圓」），試聽判斷為可接受。The same model as the previous section, but the emotion is given by its category ALONE: the continuous arousal / dominance / valence conditioning is withheld (set to nan), with the guidance coefficient still at w=1. This is not a workaround. The Mandarin ESD recordings in the training data (17,150 utterances) carry an emotion category and no continuous labels, and so does the fear/disgust expansion, so all seven categories met this condition during training. The arousal on each card is MEASURED from the output by the speech-emotion-recognition model, not a setting: no arousal was supplied here. Note also that whisper transcribed one character differently in the sad and angry clips; on listening these were judged acceptable.

強度旋鈕：引導係數 wIntensity knob: guidance coefficient w

w 為推論時的引導係數，用來放大情緒條件的影響：w=0 即一般的條件式生成，w 越大，模型越往「有情緒條件」與「無情緒條件」之間的差異方向外推。實驗中以 w ≈ 1 表現最佳，再往上雖然聽感更誇張，卻已是分佈外的外插。w is the guidance coefficient applied at inference to amplify the emotion condition. w=0 is plain conditional generation; larger w extrapolates further along the direction between the conditioned and unconditioned outputs. In our experiments w ≈ 1 works best: beyond that the effect sounds stronger but is already extrapolation outside the training data.

兩種下達情緒的方式Two ways of requesting the emotion

右欄來自第二個模型：同樣以 CosyVoice 2 為基礎，但不給 ADV 向量、也沒有引導係數，情緒只由一句固定的模板指定（generate a {emotion} version of the following sentence），以 19,854 句純中文資料微調。左欄是前兩區那個模型在 w=1 的輸出，兩欄同句、同語者、同隨機種子。以八句語意中性的文本量測：文字指令下，換情緒造成的激發度差異（標準差 0.063）大於換句子造成的差異（0.051）；ADV 條件在 w=1 則相反（0.033 對 0.046），也就是換情緒的效果比換句子還小。文字指令在預設狀態下的情緒分離度，相當於 ADV 條件推到 w=2——而後者已是分佈外的外插。The right-hand column comes from a second model, also built on CosyVoice 2, but given no ADV vector and no guidance coefficient: the emotion is carried by a fixed template (generate a {emotion} version of the following sentence) and nothing else, fine-tuned on 19,854 Mandarin-only utterances. The left-hand column is the model from the previous two sections at w=1. Same sentence, same speaker prompt, same random seed. Measured over eight semantically neutral sentences: with the text instruction, changing the emotion moves arousal more (SD 0.063) than changing the sentence does (0.051); with ADV conditioning at w=1 the reverse holds (0.033 vs 0.046) -- the emotion matters less than the sentence. The text instruction reaches, with no knob at all, the separation that ADV conditioning needs w=2 to reach, and w=2 is already extrapolation outside the training data.
